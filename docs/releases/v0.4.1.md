Leaderboards are back, your progress comes with you, setup is simpler, and players can tell us when something goes wrong.

## Play

Already on 0.4.0? The launcher updates itself. New here? Download **`launcher.exe`** (Windows) or **`launcher-linux-x86_64`** (Linux, Steam Deck) below, run it, press **Connect**, then **Play**. More in [Play in five minutes](https://github.com/JDevWebb/5th-echelon-enhanced#play-in-five-minutes).

## What's new

### Playing
- **Leaderboards and stats are back**, and they're global: the stats you earn on any server in the community network add up in one place, and the leaderboards rank everyone on the network.
- **"A different version of the game" no longer blocks joins** between copies of the game that differ, such as a Steam copy in another language and a Ubisoft Connect copy in English. The host needs 0.4.1. Hosts can turn the check back on with `AllowDataMismatch = false` in `uplay.toml`.
- **Joining public matches** hosted by a player without an open port: the server's NAT helper now helps with those joins instead of refusing them, and it no longer refuses a game that offers a VPN's address (Radmin's 26.x, say).
- **Fewer relayed sessions:** a port mapping left on the router by an earlier game no longer stops the game from mapping its port again. Before, you were relayed for the whole session.

### The launcher
- **Your progress comes with you.** When you have no 5th Echelon save yet, setup now brings over your Ubisoft Connect save (raised to rank 5 only if it's lower) instead of making a blank rank 5 profile. It looks under every Ubisoft account and edition, and inside Proton or Wine. **Settings › Save game › Import a save file** takes a save from another PC, Ubisoft Connect's `1.save`, or one of the launcher's backups.
- **A clean start for settings from older launchers.** Settings from upstream 5th Echelon or an earlier version of this launcher are backed up to `uplay.toml.old`, and the launcher starts fresh. It keeps your game version, a save folder you chose, and the accounts it set up itself; it finds your other accounts again with your identity.
- **The network adapter is automatic.** Each time the game starts, it uses whichever adapter reaches the server, so switching between Wi-Fi and Ethernet, or a leftover Radmin adapter, no longer breaks joins or shows a failed check. Groups on a VPN can still pin one in Settings.
- **A window you can resize**, maximise or make full screen (<kbd>F11</kbd>), with everything scaled to fit, so it no longer runs off small or high-DPI screens. **Settings › Display** (or Ctrl +, Ctrl − and Ctrl 0) makes text and controls bigger or smaller.
- **"How did that go?"** After a game where something went wrong (a crash, a refused sign-in or join, or matches through the relay), and about once a week otherwise, the launcher asks how it went, at most once a day. You can send your logs with your answer. Your PC's name, your user folder and your internet address are removed first, and you can read every file before it's sent. **Settings › Feedback** turns this off, or sends feedback any time.

### Steam Deck and Linux
- **Gaming Mode:** the launcher runs full screen, so every button can be clicked. Before, buttons on the right and lower parts of the window didn't respond.
- **A game moved to another library** (to or from an SD card): the launcher finds Proton's files for the game, and your save, in any Steam library. When they don't exist yet, it says where it looked and what to do.

### Running servers
- **Player management** in the admin UI: see and manage the players on every server in the network, with their play time and matches.
- **Players' reports** in the admin UI: what the player said, the logs they sent, and what the server saw of their session. Reports about problems also go to Discord or Slack (all, or none, under Alerts).
- **Global stats and leaderboards** kept by the coordinator, with a Leaderboards tab in the admin UI.
- **Metrics:** play time from real play sessions, finished matches (mode, map, length, players), failed joins and matches started. Each player's sign-in counts once (it counted twice), and Spies vs Mercs matches no longer show as co-op.

### For developers
- The client writes `bl-dataversion.txt`, what the game's data version is made of, so two players' files show why their copies differ.
- The client logs failed sign-ins.
- The release's VirusTotal scan waits out the rate limit instead of leaving an empty table.

## Good to know

- **Antivirus:** the launcher and the client DLL aren't code-signed yet. Check your download against the verification details below, and see [Antivirus warnings](https://github.com/JDevWebb/5th-echelon-enhanced#antivirus-warnings).
- **You need your own copy of the game.** Nothing of the game is included.
