# 0.4.6 "Comms Check"

Grim would never send a mission out without comms. The community network can finally talk: chat in Global, in your lobby and with friends, in the launcher and the game's overlay, and you can see who's online on your server. It reaches beyond a match: Global for everyone on the network, and messages with friends on any server, from the launcher too.

Also on this op:
- **Join** a friend's party or public match without waiting for an invite.
- **Play SvM** and **Play Coop** go straight from the launcher into the online lobby.
- **Searches no longer hang** on a match that has already started.
- **Four crashes** in the game's own code tracked down and patched.
- **A host migration that no longer spins for ever.**
- **Freezes that leave a note** of where they stuck.
- **A new Beta features section**, whose first beta is a fix for slow mouse turning above 60 fps.
- **The community network moves to a new address**, so its coordinator can fail over.

## Play

Already on 0.4.5? The launcher updates itself. New here? Download **`launcher.exe`** (Windows) or **`launcher-linux-x86_64`** (Linux, Steam Deck) below, run it, press **Connect**, then **Play**. More in [Play in five minutes](https://github.com/JDevWebb/5th-echelon-enhanced#play-in-five-minutes).

Start the game from the launcher (or through Steam with the launcher's **Start the game through Steam**): the launcher is what updates the game's add-on, and a game always started from Ubisoft Connect or Steam keeps the add-on it had until servers refuse it as outdated.

## What's new

### Chat (community network)
- **Global, Lobby and Messages**, on the launcher's new **Chat** page and the overlay's **Chat** tab. Global is everyone on the community network, on every server; Lobby is the players in your party or match; Messages are with your friends, whatever server they're on. All about it in [Chat](https://github.com/JDevWebb/5th-echelon-enhanced/blob/main/docs/chat.md).
- **Pop-ups in the game**, as **Settings › Notifications** says, per room: never, only when you're mentioned, outside matches, or always (unless you change it: Global outside matches, Lobby and friends always). Several close together show as one.
- **The rules:** no links but a Discord invite in its own form (the launcher asks before opening one); slurs and hate masked as `***` (swearing is fine); not too fast. New accounts wait 30 minutes, or a match, before posting in Global.
- **Report, block, delete:** right-click a name to report the message, block the sender, add them as a friend or message them, or delete your own. Messages are kept from 2 hours (a lobby's) to 30 days (with a friend), and the network's admins can read them; they see reports on a new **Moderation** page, and can delete a message, mute (a room or everywhere, for a time or for good) or ban. Nothing is muted automatically.
- Chat is the community network's alone: servers and networks of your own have everything else.

### Playing
- **Join a friend** (thanks to mpw, who wrote it). **Join** beside a friend, in the overlay or on the launcher's Friends card, takes you into their party or public match without waiting for an invite, and the launcher starts the game first if it isn't running. An invite-only party or a private match still needs an invite, and a match that has started can't be joined until it's over. Your game only takes a join it asked for itself, so no server can pull you anywhere. It needs your server on 0.4.6. More in [Joining a friend](https://github.com/JDevWebb/5th-echelon-enhanced/blob/main/docs/friends.md#joining-a-friend).
- **Who's online:** the launcher's Friends card lists who else is online on your server under **Online now**, with Add friend, Invite (while the game runs) and Block; the overlay's **Find players** says the same. On a friends-only server, it's your friends.
- **Lobbies past their mode's size** (thanks to PsykoGun's, BrunoMula's and Aless's reports). The overlay's **Most players** goes up to 99, and hosts use it: about a third of the play time on eu1 is in matches bigger than their mode was made for, and above it some players' games crash or freeze. It stays allowed, and the overlay now says so under **Most players** when a match is above its mode's size (2 for co-op, 4 for Classic, 6 for Frontline, 8 for Blacklist, Team Deathmatch and Capture); **Playing now** shows it too ("9/10 (made for 8)"). A Spies vs Mercs map has 5 starting spots a team, so a sixth player on a team starts elsewhere on the map. It needs your server on 0.4.6.
- **Searches say what else is open** (thanks to Pa3cio). The game only finds matches of the mode you search for, so with few players two lone hosts of different modes never met (about 20 times in a day on eu1). A search that finds nothing while matches of another mode are open now says so: "Nobody is hosting SvM Classic right now: 2 SvM Blacklist matches are open". It needs your server on 0.4.6.
- **Searches don't get stuck on a match that has started** (thanks to Aless's, Tea_bag's and PsykoGun's reports). Matchmaking handed out public matches already under way: the host's game turns a joiner away from those, or the searching game waited on one for good and had to be restarted. On eu1 one match under way kept everyone searching its mode from playing until it ended (178 stuck searches in three days). A match a join was just turned away from as started is now left out of searches for a while, so a search finds the open ones, or finds nothing and hosts a lobby the next searcher joins. It needs your server on 0.4.6.
- **A host migration that never ends leaves the match** (thanks to passiverat's and playnwork's reports). When a match's host left and nobody could take over, "Host has changed. Migrating match..." stayed up for good and the game had to be restarted: the game waits for a migration with no time limit at all. After 15 seconds the game now leaves the match the way it does when a host is lost, back to the menu, and the overlay says why.
- **Steam's launch options can't switch online play off unnoticed** (thanks to Wikowikor). A game started with `-offline` (here, in Steam's launch options for the game) never goes online. The launcher's checklist now warns when Steam's launch options have it, and the overlay says so for as long as the game runs.

### Crashes and freezes
The game's own code had bugs that crashed it, and 0.4.5's crash reports showed exactly where. The add-on now patches each one in memory as the game starts (the game's files are never changed), on DirectX 9 and 11, and leaves a game build it doesn't recognise alone. The log says when a patch does its job, so the next reports will show it working.
- **Big Spies vs Mercs lobbies** (thanks to Matt). The game keeps state slots for eight players. A ninth player and on gets state of its own, but when one of them left, its teardown also threw away a list every player shares, and the next player to spawn crashed on it (and the map's end threw it away a second time). That list is now left alone. This was the crash in rooms of 10.
- **Leaving a match** (thanks to LocoDonut). Particle effects queued for drawing could be drawn after a map load had freed what they draw into. They're skipped once it's gone (one frame of sparks, at most).
- **Unplugging a headset** (thanks to ThisMouseIsAss). When voice chat couldn't open a microphone, it deleted its audio devices without closing them, and the next headset change read a deleted one. They're now closed first, and a dead one is skipped.
- **The game's network code** (thanks to Drax333 and Aless). A sent packet could call back into something that no longer existed when its delivery was confirmed. Those callbacks are now checked first, and every copy of one is removed with it.
- **Freezes leave a note** (thanks to Tea_bag and TrustInDemon). A freeze (the picture stops, the sound plays on) throws no error, so the log just stopped. When the game draws nothing for 10 seconds with its window in front, the add-on now writes where it was stuck, and the launcher asks how it went. That's how the next freeze gets fixed.
- **The overlay can't end the game** (thanks to mpw and JeezyWeezy). On a PC where DirectX 11 wouldn't make the small test device the overlay hooks in with, the overlay crashed the game, or later went missing (no **F5**). The test device is now asked for in four ways, so the overlay should start on those PCs too; if none works, the game runs on without the overlay, and the log says why.
- `DisableCrashFixes = true` in `uplay.toml` turns these patches off, should one ever be the problem.

### Smaller fixes
- **Internet play on game builds identified long ago** (thanks to Gex). A game whose addresses the launcher's **Try to identify** found with a release before 0.4.0 had none for the internet-play fix, so it ran without it. They're now looked for when the game starts.
- **Locked out after wrong passwords** (thanks to mrb2k4). Too many failed sign-ins now says to wait 10 minutes, and that the server's admins can reset a forgotten password (on the community network, through the launcher's **Support** page); the checklist's wrong password says so too.
- **An outdated game says so.** A game the server refused (an outdated version, a ban) showed "Lost the 5th Echelon server: reconnecting", which never happened. The overlay now shows the server's own words: "Start the game from the launcher to update it".
- **A slow connection isn't a refused sign-in.** A sign-in the server never answered was logged as refused, and the launcher asked why the server hadn't let you in.
- **5th Echelon's DLL gone from the game folder** (thanks to luke). Antivirus software sometimes removes the add-on's `uplay_r1_loader.dll`, and then the game won't start at all. The checklist now says so and its fix puts it back (Play does too); if it goes again, add the game folder to your antivirus's exclusions.
- **Minimised, the overlay rests.** With the game minimised, the overlay failed to draw thousands of times a second (writing an error each time); it now skips those frames.
- **Chat's times:** the overlay's chat lines show when each was sent, as your PC's clock shows it.

### Beta features
- **A new Settings › Game › Beta features**, for changes still being tried out. Each is off unless you turn it on, may feel wrong or crash the game, and takes effect the next time the game starts. While any is on, the launcher sends the game's full log to your server's admins each time the game closes (names, folders and addresses on your PC hidden), and the Play page says so.
- **Mouse turning the same at any frame rate** (thanks to RAZA's and BEEG's reports). Above 60 fps the game turns less for the same mouse movement, so turning feels slow and many players cap their frame rate. This beta scales the mouse's turn so it turns as it does at 60 fps, whatever the frame rate. Controllers aren't touched. If turning gets *faster* above 60 fps with it on, turn it off and tell us.

### The launcher
- **Play SvM and Play Coop** (thanks to mpw, who wrote it). The arrow beside **Play** starts the game straight into the Spies vs Mercs or Co-op online lobby: no intro videos, no title screen, no key to press. The launcher remembers the choice, and the game started from Steam afterwards goes to the same lobby until you press plain **Play** again. A start for a friend's **Join** goes to the join instead, and a game that can't get online goes on as usual after its own message.
- **The community network's new address**, `scbl.jdevwebb.net`. A launcher that saved the old one (`play.scbl.jdevwebb.net`) moves to the new one by itself; typing either sets you up on the community network.

### Running servers
- **A coordinator can answer to more than one name** (`--coordinator-alias`), and member servers move to a new name without joining afresh: the coordinator at the address a server joined has to list the new name as its own (`/v1/info`). See [Moving a coordinator to a new name](https://github.com/JDevWebb/5th-echelon-enhanced/blob/main/docs/failover.md#moving-a-coordinator-to-a-new-name).
- **A proxied coordinator must answer through Cloudflare**, not just show Cloudflare's header: the installer wants `/v1/info` to answer 200, since Cloudflare's security settings blocking the servers' own addresses would break heartbeats and keep standbys from taking over.
- **Chat runs on the community network's coordinator only** (`--chat`, which the installer sets there alone), and only servers running the project's signed release pass messages. The admin UI's new **Moderation** page has the reports, the refused links, the mutes and the names that break the rules; a player's page shows their mutes, each with **Lift**.
- **Logs from beta features** arrive in the admin UI's **Reports › Automatic**, tagged `beta` and the beta's name. They come once each time a game closes with a beta on, up to 12 a day a player, apart from the player's own 5 reports.
- **The game's sessions in its diagnostics:** the Sessions page shows each change of a game's match and lobby sessions ("Online sessions: match open, lobby locked, in a match"). These lines are for finding what marks a Spies vs Mercs round under way, so that started matches can be left out of searches from the host's own word.
- **Backups are snapshots:** each server's databases go to R2 as a snapshot every 2 hours (unchanged ones skipped, a week kept), instead of Litestream's live copy, which sent every change and ran past R2's free requests. `install-server.sh --backups-only` switches an existing server. A failover now restores a snapshot up to 2 hours old, so run `backup.sh snapshot` before a planned takeover.
- **A stray reply is named in the log.** A game's reply where the server expects a request is logged with what it answers, not only its size.

## Updating to 0.4.6

**Servers of your own:** update as usual (the rollout, in an update window, or the installer by hand). Nothing else is needed unless you move your coordinator to a new name: then follow [Moving a coordinator to a new name](https://github.com/JDevWebb/5th-echelon-enhanced/blob/main/docs/failover.md#moving-a-coordinator-to-a-new-name).

**The community network** (in this order; each step can be undone until the record is proxied):
1. **Before 0.4.6's launcher goes out:** an A record for `scbl.jdevwebb.net`, **DNS only**, pointing at eu1. 0.4.6 launchers reach the community network by that name (Support, the roadmap, the server list).
2. **In the 0.4.6 update window, on eu1 (the coordinator's machine):** the installer with `--coordinator-domain scbl.jdevwebb.net --coordinator-alias play.scbl.jdevwebb.net`. The coordinator then answers to both names, each with its own certificate, and runs chat; players' signed requests made for either name count, so 0.4.5 launchers' Support keeps working.
3. **Each server (eu1, na1, oceania):** the installer with `--coordinator https://scbl.jdevwebb.net`. Each asks the coordinator at `play.scbl` whether the new name is its own, then joins again with its secret.
4. **Check:** heartbeats from every server (the admin UI's Servers page), friends across servers, Support from a 0.4.5 and a 0.4.6 launcher, the roadmap, chat between two servers, and `curl -s https://scbl.jdevwebb.net/v1/info` listing both names.
5. **After the release, failover on:** an Origin certificate for `scbl.jdevwebb.net` on every server, the record switched to **proxied** with SSL/TLS Full (strict), and from each server `curl -sI https://scbl.jdevwebb.net/v1/info` answering 200 through Cloudflare (allow the servers' addresses if Cloudflare blocks them). Then `--standby` on the servers and a drill, as in [failover.md](https://github.com/JDevWebb/5th-echelon-enhanced/blob/main/docs/failover.md). `play.scbl.jdevwebb.net` stays DNS only on eu1 and doesn't follow a takeover.
6. **Once no launcher older than 0.4.6 signs in** (servers make launchers update, so within days): the installer on eu1 with `--coordinator-alias -`, and remove the `play.scbl` record.
