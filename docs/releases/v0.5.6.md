# 0.5.6 "Fair Game"

**0.5.6 is about playing fair and getting in.** Joining a friend or a listed match no longer needs an invitation, quick match fills matches that are short of players first, in-match chat can be muted and goes with reports, fair play reads more of the game and stops flagging what isn't cheating, and a run of crashes and connection problems are fixed.

## Getting into matches

- **Join without an invitation.** **Join** on a friend in a public match (in the launcher or the overlay), or on a match in the Matches tab, takes you straight in, even when its round is under way. The server keeps the match for your game for 4 minutes and your game joins it by itself; the host no longer sends you an invitation. Parties and private matches still need one. (Thanks to MPW1412, PR #18.)
- **Quick match fills matches first.** Matches under way with a free seat come before lobbies still waiting for players, nearest first; among hosts within 30 ms of each other the fuller one comes first. A match raised past its mode's size (Blacklist's 8, say) comes after the lobbies: still found, never refused.
- **A full match says so.** Joining a friend whose match or party has every seat taken now says *That player's match is full - try again when a seat opens*, instead of letting you in to fail in the game.
- **A refused Join no longer starts the game.** The launcher asks the server first and starts the game only once the join is accepted.
- **Two PCs behind one router** whose home network doesn't carry the game now meet through the relay instead of failing every join.
- **Restarting the game with a join pending** no longer counts as a failed join or takes you out of that match.
- **The HTTPS tunnel takes over only when it's needed.** It waited 1 second for the relay to register your game; on a far relay that was too little, and players whose connection worked went through the tunnel. Once the relay has answered it now waits 3 seconds.
- **A tunnel that keeps being cut** slows its reconnects and tells you, instead of signing in again every few seconds.
- **Signing in through a relay's tunnel** now gives the others your real address. Before, the server took the relay's address for yours, and your game's checked address wasn't used.

## Chat and reports

- **Mute in-match chat.** The overlay's Match tab mutes a player's chat for the rest of the match, players you've blocked are never shown, and **Settings › Game** sets the in-match chat to everyone, your team only, or off.
- **Reports carry the chat.** Reporting a player from the overlay sends the admins that player's in-match chat lines your game showed, even when a cheat tool showed them under another name.

## Fair play

All checks are report only, as before: nothing is done to a player automatically.

- **Less noise.** A stock weapon's own switches, co-op's invisible/unhittable states while downed or in a cutscene, and settings a host chose (a custom time limit) are no longer reported.
- **Names.** Each game reads the session's player list and notes when it shows a player under another name than their account's (the cheat table's name changer).
- **Weapons and attachments.** Attachments' stats are read and compared between players too.
- **Health.** A character's health above its maximum is noted.
- **Teamkills** are counted per match and shown with the witness reports.

## Replays and results

- **Recordings are kept 30 days** for the admins to review reported matches: players' characters only, named. `ReplayUpload = false` in `uplay.toml` keeps them on your PC.
- **Match results** (rounds and the winner) are kept with each match for the admins' Matches page.

## Fixed

- **Co-op with more than two players:** a crash when a player joined, left or was removed (a character's ragdoll), and one when a player who'd left was still being moved.
- **Customize Gear after a match** no longer crashes the game, and after a quick start it offers the Spy and Merc characters, not the campaign's loadout.
- **The intro videos** are skipped on every start, not only with the quick start: some games stood still for 10 seconds or more while they played.
- **Alt+Enter on DirectX 9** can freeze the game for a minute or more inside the graphics driver (the game's own, with or without 5th Echelon): you're told once, with what helps.
- **A match past its mode's size:** every player is told once that games crash more often above it.
- **Crashes in the logs you send** with a report now reach the admins' Crashes page.

## For server admins

- **Chat transcripts:** **Chat and names › Transcript** (and a player's **Chat** button) shows every kept message in every room, deleted ones too; reading a player's chat or a direct conversation goes to the audit log.
- **The Sessions page** has the name witnesses, teamkills, attachment and health notes, tunnel opens and closes (one line each), and address changes.
- **Relay coverage:** every launcher's ping to every relay is kept by place, for seeing where players are far from a relay.
- **JoinNow and JoinRoom** take `direct` (no invitation into a public match) and refuse a full match.
- **The bot tests** resend an unanswered handshake packet, as the game does.

## Updating to 0.5.6

**Players:** the launcher updates by itself. **Servers of your own:** update as usual. As with every release, a server on 0.5.6 takes 0.5.6 games and launchers only.
