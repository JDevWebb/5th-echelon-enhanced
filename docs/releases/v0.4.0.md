The first release of 5th Echelon Enhanced: free community servers for *Splinter Cell: Blacklist* multiplayer, and a launcher that sets the game up for you. Co-op and Spies vs Mercs, with no VPN and no port forwarding.

## Play

1. Download **`launcher.exe`** (Windows) or **`launcher-linux-x86_64`** (Linux, Steam Deck) below.
2. Run it. It finds your game, and under **Choose a server** picks the community server closest to you: Europe, North America or Oceania.
3. Press **Connect**, pick the name other players will see, then **Play**. Press <kbd>F5</kbd> in the game for friends and invites.

**Used upstream 5th Echelon before?** Use this launcher instead of the old one; there's nothing to uninstall. It replaces the old client in your game folder (keeping the game's original file), and your settings and saves carry over. Your old server stays selected: to play on the community servers, open **Servers** and press **Switch** on the closest. Played over Radmin VPN or Hamachi? You don't need it here, and the Status card fixes the network adapter setting it left behind. More in [Play in five minutes](https://github.com/JDevWebb/5th-echelon-enhanced#play-in-five-minutes).

## What's in it

**For players**
- **Co-op and Spies vs Mercs** on the community servers: Find Teammate, Quick Match, lobby invites and invites into private matches.
- **Internet play without a VPN:** the server tells your game its public address, your router is asked to open the game's port, and when neither gets through, the match goes through the server's relay.
- **A launcher that does the setup:** finds the game, installs the client, makes your account and a rank 5 save so co-op and Spies vs Mercs are unlocked, and shows a Status card with a fix for anything that's wrong, including a note when a VPN is in the way.
- **One identity, every server:** no password to remember. Your friends, blocks and name follow you to every server in the community network.
- **Friends in the game:** an overlay (<kbd>F5</kbd>) with your friends and what they're playing, friend requests, player search, blocking and invites.
- **Updates on their own:** the launcher updates itself from signed releases, and servers refuse outdated clients, so everyone stays on a version that works together.
- **Linux and Steam Deck:** a native Linux launcher, and Proton and Wine support in the client.

**For server operators**
- **One script** for a server on any small Linux VPS, with automatic HTTPS, sandboxed services and `harden-host.sh` for the machine.
- **A network of your own:** a coordinator shares friends between your servers, lists them in a directory, and rolls out each signed release to them.
- **An admin UI:** players, cities, pings, bandwidth, alerts to Discord or Slack, and the update rollout.

**Fixed in testing, before this release**
- Co-op missions failed to load when a player went through the relay: the relay dropped the game's largest packets. Confirmed working with one and with both players relayed.
- Find Teammate could drop you into someone's private co-op match. Private matches are now never offered to matchmaking.
- Hosts whose router doesn't forward a port couldn't be joined. They now go through the relay.
